Looking ahead

The future of software engineering

In the next 5–10 years, AI will take on more repetitive coding, testing and debugging. Our team expects engineers to spend more time shaping problems, designing systems and reviewing what AI builds. The tools will change; thoughtful decisions, accountability and creativity will matter even more.
