## What I think about AI in software engineering today

I think AI has changed software engineering quite a bit in the last couple of years. From my experience as a full-stack developer between 2024 and 2026, it made development much easier and faster. Things like debugging, writing boilerplate code, understanding unfamiliar codebases, exploring new technologies, and implementing features became significantly less time-consuming. I personally use LLMs as part of my regular development workflow, and they have become almost like another developer to discuss problems and solutions with.

## Where software engineering will be in 5–10 years

I think software engineering will become much more AI-driven in the next 5–10 years. Developers will spend less time writing code manually and more time defining problems, designing systems, reviewing AI-generated code, and making architectural decisions. AI agents might handle entire development tasks, from understanding requirements to implementation, testing, and deployment, with developers supervising the process.

## My hot take on AI

AI is making coding easier, but knowing what not to let AI do is becoming the real skill.
