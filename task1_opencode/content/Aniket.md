## What I think about AI in software engineering today

AI has made software development faster and more accessible, especially for debugging, learning and generating code. However, it can still produce incorrect solutions, so developers need to understand and verify what it generates. I see AI as a helpful assistant rather than a replacement for software engineering knowledge.

## Where software engineering will be in 5–10 years

I think AI agents will handle more routine coding, testing and debugging tasks. Software engineers will increasingly focus on system design, problem-solving, architecture and supervising AI-generated solutions. Understanding how to work effectively with AI will become an important developer skill.

## My hot take on AI

AI won't replace developers, but developers who use AI effectively may replace those who don't.
