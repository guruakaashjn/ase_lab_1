## What I think about AI in software engineering today

AI has become a powerful tool for accelerating software development, from code generation and debugging to testing and documentation. However, it can also produce convincing but incorrect solutions, so understanding the underlying concepts and being able to critically evaluate its output are more important than ever.

## Where software engineering will be in 5–10 years

Software engineering will likely become increasingly AI-assisted, with developers spending less time writing repetitive code and more time on architecture, problem-solving and system design. AI agents may independently handle entire development tasks, but human judgment, creativity and accountability will remain essential.

## My hot take on AI

The best software engineers of the future won't be those who write the most code, but those who know what code shouldn't be written.
