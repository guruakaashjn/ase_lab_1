## What I think about AI in software engineering today

The best software engineers of tomorrow won't necessarily be those who write the most code. They'll be those who understand problems deeply, make sound engineering decisions, and know how to leverage AI without blindly depending on it.

## Where software engineering will be in 5–10 years

I believe their will huge transformations over the next 5-10 years. Software Engineering will not disappear, rather the definition of what it means to be a software engineer will fundamentally change. From writing code to engineering systems, and architecting software and security becoming more and more important and crucial. We are here to see a drastic shift in the software engineering domain. On the same side, their will be a lot of new things for us to learn and apply in our day-to-day life activities.

## My hot take on AI

Use AI to amplify your engineering capabilities, not outsource your engineering thinking.
