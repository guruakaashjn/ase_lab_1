A shared perspective

The Future of Software Engineering

Across our team, there is a clear expectation that software engineering will become more AI-assisted—not less dependent on engineering judgment. As agents take on more routine coding, testing, debugging and even end-to-end tasks, developers will have more room to define the real problem, shape the architecture and make deliberate decisions about what should be built.

That shift makes critical review more important, not less. AI can accelerate learning and implementation, but generated code still needs human attention for correctness, security and maintainability. Creativity, accountability and a deep understanding of systems remain essential as the tools change.

Design the system

Spend less time on repetition and more on architecture, constraints and the problem worth solving.

Review with intent

Understand AI-generated work, check its assumptions and own the quality of the result.

Keep learning

Use AI to extend engineering capability while keeping human judgment firmly in the loop.
